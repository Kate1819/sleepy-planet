# Sleepy Planet

Earth is fast asleep in her nightcap, and cheeky stars keep tumbling toward her. Launch rockets to pop them so she can keep dreaming.

**Play:** https://kate1819.github.io/sleepy-planet/

- Tap or click anywhere to launch a rocket. Hold to keep firing.
- Two ways to play: **Story** (5 hearts) or **Dreamy** (endless hearts).
- 14 nights, each bringing a new kind of star: Embers, Comets, Twin Stars, Bubble Stars, Blue Giants, Hopstars, Popcorn Stars, Spiral Stars, Pulsars, the Star Caterpillar, Fireflies, Cloud Mother, the Hungry Dark and the Eclipse. Endless dream nights after that.
- Five rockets: Spark, Firework, Frost, Seekers, Supernova.
- Six skills: Star Blanket, Star Friends, Lullaby, Solar Wind, Moon Guard, Starstorm.
- Every star you pop plays a note in the lullaby and lights up a constellation.
- Keys: 1-5 rockets, Q W E R T Y skills, P pause, M music.
- Works on desktop and phone. One HTML file, no libraries.
